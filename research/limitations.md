# Limitations
This is an educational library, not production-grade. Small synthetic tasks, three seeds, no external benchmarks, no claims about real-world performance. No full tensor autodiff, pooling, batch normalization, recurrent layers, padding/stride options, serialization, GPU, or hardware timing. Layers cache their last forward pass and require matching backward calls; do not share a layer instance across graph branches. Optimizer state is bound to persistent parameter arrays. L2 is coupled weight regularization including biases, not decoupled AdamW. Extreme numerical magnitudes and invalid shape/parameter inputs need further audit. PyTorch is a reference dependency only. Docker and lint are not verified. No clinical or peer-review claims.
